Key Takeaways


  1. What is a text structure?
    A text structure organises information according to a logical pattern, helping us comprehend and remember what we read and write more clearly ourselves.

  2. What are the three major text structures?
    We know that the three major text structures are Compare and Contrast, Cause and Effect, and Problem and Solution.

  3. What is a compare and contrast structure?
    A compare and contrast structure explains how two or more subjects are similar, different, or both.

  4. What is a cause and effect structure?
    A cause and effect structure shows how one event leads to another event or result, helping us understand consequences.

  5. How do we identify a cause and effect structure?
    We can ask "Why did this happen?" to find the cause, and "What happened because of this?" to find the effect.

  6. What are the three forms a cause and effect structure can take?
    Cause and effect text structure can show a single cause with multiple effects, multiple causes producing a single effect, or a whole chain of events.

  7. What is a problem and solution structure?
    We know that a problem and solution structure identifies a difficulty and the response made to address it, flowing logically from one to the other.
